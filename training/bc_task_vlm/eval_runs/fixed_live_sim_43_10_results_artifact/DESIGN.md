# Dashboard design

## Intent
Monitor error-free live-sim success across current 43/10 checkpoints and communication modes.

## Layout
Summary-first: metric definition, native comparison, migrated communication comparison, then exact tables and coverage.

## Visual system
Neutral comparison bars, consistent split colors, percent axes, and compact exact-result tables.

## Data states
Partial while scheduled evaluations are incomplete; missing checkpoints are labeled pending rather than shown as zero.
